01

First Principles

Strip to bedrock truths

Before mechanics, before money — what is irreducibly true about this game? The verb isn't locate. It's align.

The Loop
SEE → SEEK → ALIGN → MATCH

The verb is ALIGN, not "locate." Build everything around alignment, never around coordinates.

The Heartbeat
A 0–100% match score

On-device image-feature matching compares the hunter's live camera against the target photo. The score is the game's pulse.

The Magic
Live hotter / colder

An augmented viewfinder shows a 30% ghost of the target; align the ghost with reality to win — the single most delightful mechanic.

The Twist
Withholding GPS is the fun

The scarcity of coordinates forces human vision + clues to do the navigating. The weakness is the design.

Fair by Nature
Spoof-resistant by design

You must physically reproduce a perceptual viewpoint — so anti-cheat is baked into the core mechanic (unlike pure-GPS games).

The Atom
Everything is a "Spot"

{ anchor photo, area polygon, clues, difficulty, hints[] } — one tidy object the whole game is built from.

Minimum Viable Magic
Hide → Find → Confirm

One person hides a spot, another finds it, the app confirms the match. If that single loop feels good, the game works.

Zero Content Cost
The world is the board

Players generate every location — a content perpetual-motion machine with no operator authoring burden.

Substitute

Motion Caches

Swap the still photo for a 3-second video pan — reproduce the camera motion, not just a frame.

Audio Caches

Swap sight for sound — record a spot's ambient soundscape (a fountain, a train crossing) and hunt by ear.

AI Starter Spots

Swap the human creator for AI — auto-generate synthetic spots from Street View so no region launches empty.

Combine

PhotoSpot Trek

Combine with fitness: distance walked + spots found = a combined score. Bridges to the Family Fitness app in the series.

Narrative Trails

Combine with storytelling: chain spots so each find reveals the next clue plus a paragraph — a neighborhood becomes a choose-your-path novel.

AR Reward Drops

Combine with AR: nailing the alignment spawns an AR object anchored exactly there, visible only from that viewpoint.

Adapt

The Daily Spot

Adapt the Wordle pattern: one shared daily spot, share your score, build streaks.

Passport Stamps

Adapt letterboxing: every spot has a unique stamp/sigil you collect in a passport.

Modify / Magnify

Difficulty Tiers

Plaza (easy, wide area) → Alley (medium) → Needle (brutal: a crack in one brick, a reflection in one window).

Area-Precision Dial

A creator-set difficulty dial: a generous 500m radius for beginners vs. a cruel "somewhere in this district" for experts.

Put to Use

Tourism

Boards sponsor spots to drive foot traffic to hidden gems — "find our town's best-kept secret viewpoint."

Education

"Then-and-now" caches: the target is a historical photo; find where it was taken and see how the place changed.

Accessibility

Spots documenting wheelchair-accessible viewpoints — the hunt crowdsources accessibility data.

Eliminate

Blind Mode

Eliminate GPS entirely: only clues + visual matching, no map dot — pure detective mode.

Zen Mode

Eliminate the timer: wander and find spots serendipitously. Two moods — Race vs. Stroll.

Reverse

GeoGuessr Flip

Reverse it: given a photo, guess where in the world it is, then go verify in person.

Bounty Mode

Reverse hider/seeker: seekers request a view ("photograph the top of X hill") and hiders fulfill it.

Cross-Pollination

Steal moves from distant worlds

Borrow the proven mechanics of casinos, ERs, beekeepers, Netflix, NatGeo, and museums — and bend each toward the hunt.

🎰
Casino
  • Jackpot spots: variable-ratio rewards — rare drops unpredictably; the slot-machine pull sustains hunting.
  • Near-miss feedback: a gentle "SO close!" at 85% alignment to trigger one-more-try (used ethically).
  • Loyalty tiers: frequent hunters unlock cosmetic map themes and exclusive spot access.
🏥
ER Triage
  • Flow-state matchmaking: read player skill from past scores; serve spots at the edge of their ability.
  • "Stabilize first" onboarding: the first spot is impossible to fail (your own doorstep) — a guaranteed dopamine hit.
🐝
Beekeeping
  • Swarm-sourced hints: finders leave a directional "waggle" breadcrumb for the next hunter — emergent collective trails.
  • Pollination boost: finding a spot raises its visibility; unfound spots get a boost so nothing stays lost forever.
📺
Netflix
  • "Spots For You": a personalized feed ranked by taste — water views, street art, rooftops.
  • "Because you found X": recommendations chaining you through a city.
🌍
NatGeo
  • Light-gated spots: a cherry-blossom spot active only in spring; a golden-hour spot — teaches you to see light.
  • Migrating spots: caches tied to things that move on a schedule (a food truck, a tide pool) — time-sensitive hunts.
🏛️
Museum
  • Curated exhibitions: themed collections by guest curators — "Hidden Reflections of Chicago."
  • Provenance: each spot shows its first-finder and a find-history guestbook.

05

Anti-Solution

Every way to fail → flipped into a safeguard

List every way to sink the game, then invert each failure into a design that floats it. The strongest defenses were born here.

Fail

Impossible-to-find spots frustrate everyone.

→
Fix

Hunters rate clue quality; low-rated spots auto-hide until the creator improves them.

Fail

Trivially cheatable by screenshotting a find.

→
Fix

Liveness check — capture via live camera with gyro orientation + rough GPS + timestamp. Compass must match the target's heading, not just a matching image.

Fail

Sends people into danger — private property, cliffs, tracks.

→
Fix

Creators attest public/legal access; auto-reject spots on private parcels or near rail/water hazards; a "safe approach" indicator + no-trespass pledge before reveal; community flagging.

Fail

Dead-empty map in a new city.

→
Fix

AI-seeded starter spots from Street View so every city launches non-empty; "Founder" status + bonus rewards for the first creators in a virgin area.

Fail

Creepy / stalkery misuse — luring someone.

→
Fix

No spots near schools/homes, no spot-tied private messaging, rapid takedown on report.

Fail

Boring once you've found everything nearby.

→
Fix

Procedurally infinite AI-proposed spots + a travel mode surfacing spots in new cities. UGC supply scales with the player base — Waze/Wikipedia dynamics.

Fail

Battery/data drain from constant camera + GPS.

→
Fix

On-device lightweight matching + a low-power "cold trail" mode that wakes the camera only within range.

  • Historical-society mode: overlay a 1920 photo, find the modern vantage, blend old + new.
  • Personal Time Machine: revisit your own old spots years later; watch your life's backdrop change.
🕯️ Ritual
  • Pilgrimage collections: linked spots completed as a journey with a completion certificate.
  • Solstice / equinox special spots with ritual timing.
🤝 Community Economy
  • Spot Clubs with neighborhood leaderboards and club-vs-club seasons.
  • Creator reputation: beloved curators get followers — "follow this curator for new drops."
  • Community-funded bounties to get a hard-to-reach spot documented.
🏰 Business Moat
  • The dataset is the moat: human-curated georeferenced viewpoints — uniquely valuable for AR, mapping, tourism, ML — compounding over time.
  • B2B license of anonymized "best viewpoints" data.
  • Freemium pro tier: advanced stats, unlimited hints, offline city packs.
  • Sponsored spots ending at a local cafe ("show this find for a free coffee") — foot traffic + revenue share.
🎨 Branding
  • Names: Parallax · Vantage · EarthLens · Spotted · Re:View · Frame Hunt · Same Spot.

The Develop Phase

Synthesis

104 ideas converged on seven truths. The first one is the whole game.

🎯

The Irreducible Magic

The live ghost-overlay alignment viewfinder + a 0–100% match score. It is simultaneously the MVP, the demo money-shot, and the viral reveal GIF. Build THIS first; everything else is amplification.

🛡️

The Genius Is the Constraint

Withholding GPS precision is what makes the game both fun (human vision must navigate) and cheat-proof (you must physically reproduce a viewpoint). The apparent weakness vs. Pokémon Go is the entire moat — fun and fairness flow from the same choice.

♾️

Content Perpetual-Motion Machine

Players generate infinite levels; the operator only curates + AI-seeds empty regions. Supply scales with the player base (Waze/Wikipedia dynamics), and the accumulated viewpoint dataset becomes a compounding, defensible moat.

💞

The Emotional Layer Is the Elevator

"Memory caches" and generational hand-downs — a dad leaving a spot for his daughter to find in ten years — turn a game into something meaningful. And it's the perfect narrative spine for the father-daughter YouTube series. The demo app and the emotional story are the same thing.

👀

The Brand Soul

The real product is renewed attention — re-enchanting the ordinary world, an antidote to doom-scrolling. "Stop scrolling. Start spotting." This is both the marketing and the meaning.

🧱

Buildable on the Series Stack

MVP = Next.js + Supabase + Vercel, with a CLIP-embedding similarity call doing the "match." It satisfies the series' "easy to build" constraint while being something that doesn't exist yet — exactly the confidence-bomb the prior session wanted: build something real that's never been made, on camera.
