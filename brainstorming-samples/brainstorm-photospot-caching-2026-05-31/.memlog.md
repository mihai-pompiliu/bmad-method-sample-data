- (technique) started First Principles Thinking — strip to bedrock truths of the app
- (idea) Core loop is SEE -> SEEK -> ALIGN -> MATCH; the verb is ALIGN, not 'locate'. Build everything around alignment, not coordinates
- (idea) The match score is the game's heartbeat: a 0-100% alignment score from on-device image feature matching comparing the hunter's live camera to the target photo
- (idea) Live hotter/colder as you move: an augmented viewfinder shows a 30% ghost of the target; align the ghost with reality to win (the single most delightful mechanic)
- (idea) Deliberately withholding GPS precision IS the fun: the scarcity of coordinates forces human vision + clues to do the navigating
- (idea) Spoof-resistant by design: you must physically reproduce a perceptual viewpoint, so anti-cheat is baked into the core mechanic (unlike pure-GPS games like Pokemon Go)
- (idea) Atomic content unit = a 'Spot' object: {anchor photo, general-area polygon, creator clues, difficulty, hints[]}. Everything is a Spot
- (idea) Minimum viable magic: one person hides a spot, another finds it, app confirms the match. If that single loop feels good, the game works; everything else is amplification
- (idea) Zero content cost to operator: the world is the board and players generate all locations — a content perpetual-motion machine
- (technique) started SCAMPER Method — Substitute/Combine/Adapt/Modify/Put-to-use/Eliminate/Reverse on the core mechanic
