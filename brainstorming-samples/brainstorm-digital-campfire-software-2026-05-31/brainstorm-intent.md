# Brainstorm Intent — The Digital Campfire

## Topic
Software products (apps, tools, services) that make the internet feel cozy, warm, slow, human, and intimate again — a deliberate antidote to algorithmic feeds, infinite scroll, engagement-farming, and performative social media. Presence over broadcast; warmth over reach.

## Goal
Identify the strongest buildable "digital campfire" concept(s): core mechanics, the emotional hook, what keeps each cozy rather than addictive, a coziness-safe business model, and one MVP an indie team can ship.

## Session
Solo autonomous ideation. 62 ideas across 4 techniques: First Principles, Quantum Tunneling, Sci-Fi Artifact From the Future, Indigenous & Traditional Wisdom. Full record in `.memlog.md`; keepsake in `brainstorm.html`.

## Recommended direction (build this)

### Hearth — a care-loop social app
A single private circle of up to **7 people** gathered around a shared "fire" that **exists only while it is tended.** The flame visibly dims to embers as hours pass with no one present and **dies (archives) after ~7 days untended.** You return not to consume content but so the fire doesn't go out. This is the core inversion: an **engagement loop made of care, not addiction.**

**MVP feature set (small surface, deeply novel feel):**
- One circle, hard-capped at 7 seats. You invite someone only by **giving up your own seat for a night** (growth gated by genuine sacrifice).
- A shared fire that dims/dies if untended — care is the loop.
- The daily **Log**: one genuine voice-or-text contribution that **burns after** (no stacked archive to perform for).
- **Single-flame presence**: lit/unlit only. No read receipts, no last-seen, and **no counts anywhere** (followers/likes/views/streaks cannot be rendered — a hard architectural constraint).
- **Ember messages** that cool from bright to faint to ash over ~48h.
- A posted **Closing Time** each night (the app shuts and reopens at dawn — abolishes FOMO and 24/7 availability).
- Voice-first, dark, warm, "lit-from-below" UI.

**Coziness-safe business model (defer past MVP):**
- **Free to sit, pay to host** (own the cabin, not the conversation; hosting is a status of care).
- **Giftable firewood** — the only in-app spend is logs you give away to keep others' fires lit. Money is always an act of care, never consumption.
- No ads. E2E encrypted, zero content held; the moat is craft and ritual, not data.

## Two adjacent directions the same brand/tech grows into

**B. Fire-Keeper hospitality network.** Human hosts (paid or volunteer) staff public "bonfires" and 2am quiet fires for the lonely/grieving (the "Night Fire-Keepers" wedge). Solves the empty-room cold-start with hospitality instead of an algorithm. The most defensible standalone business; clear eldercare and late-night payers. Big Tech structurally cannot copy a warm human host.

**C. Ambient presence object.** A ceramic "ember-stone" or a "Mantel" room light that glows when a distant friend/family circle gathers. Sells presence as a one-time lovely object, sidestepping the attention economy. Strong phase-2 companion to A.

## Load-bearing principles (constraints for downstream planning)
1. **Care-loop, not attention-loop** — the product's reason-to-return must be tending something shared, not consuming a feed.
2. **No visible numbers, ever** — warmth shown as glow/heat only. One leaked metric collapses the coziness.
3. **Trust via rhythm, not availability** — "reliably here, then gone" (Closing Time, dawn/dusk check-ins, seasonal gatherings) beats "always on."
4. **Scarcity of presence** — small fixed seat counts, ephemerality, and present-only interaction (never browse the absent).
5. **Hospitality is the moat** — human welcome (onboarding, conflict-repair "mending circles," night hosts) is the durable, uncopyable edge.
6. **Monetization = generosity** — every dollar must be an act of care (host a circle, gift firewood), never extraction.

## Supporting mechanics worth keeping in the backlog
Talking-stick turn-taking (one voice at a time, enforced); Pōwhiri welcome (no silent lurking); Ubuntu co-written profiles (others describe you, you can't self-curate); Potlatch give-to-rise status; seasonal/daylight-driven usage rhythm; anti-Wrapped depth keepsake (printed, never shareable); legacy "memory log" sealed after a death; handshake-rooted ties (in-person NFC verification).

## Assumptions
- Headless solo session; no human input available — all ideas and selections generated by the agent.
