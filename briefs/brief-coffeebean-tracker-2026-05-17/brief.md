# CoffeeBean Tracker

A personal log for the beans you buy, brew, and remember.

## Problem
I keep buying coffee beans and forgetting which roaster, roast date, or brew method produced a cup I loved. Every reorder is a guess.

## For Whom
Solo home-brew coffee hobbyists who want a private record of what they have tried.

## What It Is
A lightweight single-user app (Mac menubar or iOS, undecided) for logging each bag, the brews made from it, and a quick taste impression. Local-only, no cloud, no sharing.

## Key Capabilities
- Log a bag: roaster, origin, roast date, purchase date, price.
- Log a brew against a bag: method, 1-5 rating, one-line tasting note.
- Auto-track bag lifespan (open to empty).
- Browse and filter past bags by rating or roaster.

## Success Signal
I stop re-buying beans I did not actually like, and can name my top three roasters from the last six months.

## Open Questions

- Is a favorites flag enough, or do I need tags and search?
