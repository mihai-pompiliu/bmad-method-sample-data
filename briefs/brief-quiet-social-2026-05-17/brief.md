# QuietSocial

## The Problem

Social media is exhausting. The average user opens Instagram 14 times a day, scrolls through content from people they barely know, and walks away feeling worse than when they started. The platforms are optimized for time-on-app, not for the relationships that actually matter.

For people who don't enjoy performing online, today's social apps offer a bad deal: shout into the void or miss out entirely. The people who feel this most acutely are introverts, who tell us in interview after interview that they want to stay in touch with their closest friends without the noise, the metrics, and the constant pressure to post.

## Target User

Our primary user is the quiet majority: introverts aged 25-45 who feel drained by current social platforms. They have between 5 and 15 people they genuinely care about staying connected with. They are not trying to build an audience. They are not trying to go viral. They want a calm place to share small moments with the people who already love them.

These users have tried digital detoxes. They have deleted apps and reinstalled them. They are looking for a sustainable middle ground.

## Vision

QuietSocial is a social app for people who hate social apps.

No algorithms. No feeds. No notifications. Just genuine connection with the 5 to 10 people who matter most.

We believe the future of social is smaller, slower, and more honest. We are building the app we wish existed: one that respects your attention, protects your privacy, and disappears when you close it.

## Key Capabilities

1. **Inner Circle.** Each user designates up to 10 close connections. That's the cap. No follower counts, no public profiles, no discoverability.

2. **Moments.** Share a photo, a thought, or a short voice note. It goes only to your Inner Circle. It expires after 7 days unless saved by the recipient.

3. **AI-curated weekly digest.** Every Sunday morning, our model assembles a gentle summary of what your closest friends have been up to that week, surfacing the moments most likely to matter to you.

4. **Smart notification rollups.** Instead of pinging you in real time, QuietSocial batches gentle nudges into one calm daily summary, delivered at a time you choose.

5. **Creator Mode (beta).** For Gen Z creators looking for a less-performative platform to grow their audience, Creator Mode unlocks a public-facing profile, a follower count, and a shareable link. Early data shows strong adoption among micro-influencers in the 1K to 50K range.

6. **No ads. Ever.** The business model is simple. (See below.)

## Why Now

Three things have changed. First, the post-pandemic loneliness crisis has made people more aware of the difference between contact and connection. Second, the major platforms have lost the plot, optimizing for short video and creator economy at the expense of the average user. Third, on-device AI has matured to the point where we can do meaningful curation without sending data to a server.

The window is open. Bluesky proved people will move. Mastodon proved people will tolerate friction for principles. We think the next wave is people who want to use social media less, not differently.

## Constraints and Assumptions

- iOS first, Android in month 8.
- Two-person founding team, technical and design.
- 18 months of runway at current burn.
- We assume users will cap their Inner Circle voluntarily and not lobby for higher limits.
- We assume on-device AI is sufficient for the digest feature in v1.

## Business Model

Free forever. We are not selling your data and we don't need ads.

Premium subscribers ($8/month) get access to AI summaries, priority support, and group features. We expect roughly 4 to 6 percent of active users to convert to premium within the first year, which models out to a sustainable business at our target scale.

## Success Signals

- We win when our power users have 5 to 10 close connections and open the app about twice a week. Heavy usage is a failure state, not a goal.
- Net Promoter Score above 60 within the introvert segment.
- 10M monthly active users in year two, driven by influencer-led growth loops and Creator Mode referrals.
- Average session length under 4 minutes.
- Less than 1 percent of users hit the Inner Circle cap and request a raise.

## Open Questions

- What happens when a user's Inner Circle outgrows the cap? Do we allow tiers, or hold the line?
- How do we acquire users without paid social, which feels off-brand?
- Is the weekly digest a delight or a creep factor? Early testers split.
- Should Creator Mode live in the same app, or graduate to a separate product?
