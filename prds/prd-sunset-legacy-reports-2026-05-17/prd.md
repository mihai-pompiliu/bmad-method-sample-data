## Background

Legacy Reports is the original reporting module in Threadline. It was built in 2017 on a separate Rails 4 stack, runs on its own dedicated EC2 fleet (12 instances across two regions), uses a separate auth provider integration (the old SAML bridge), and requires power users to log in a second time after entering the main app. It exposes a saved-report builder, scheduled email exports, and CSV download.

We continue to maintain it because customers have, in aggregate, 4,200 saved report definitions across 180 tenants. Active usage is much smaller: roughly 80 monthly active users across the entire customer base. Internal cost to keep it running is approximately $340K per year, split between two engineers' partial time, on-call burden, infra spend on the dedicated fleet, and the cost of keeping the old SAML bridge alive.

The Insights module has been in GA for 14 months. It is the supported reporting surface for new customers and for every feature shipped in the last two years. Roughly 30% of saved Legacy Reports map cleanly to an Insights equivalent today. The remaining 70% need either a rebuild in Insights' query builder or a one-time custom export. We have a migration tool ready for the clean-map cases and a Customer Success playbook for the rest.

We are sunsetting Legacy Reports because the cost of carrying two reporting stacks is no longer justified by the value of the older one, and because keeping the old SAML bridge alive is becoming a security liability. We are doing it carefully because the 80 active users tend to be high-value: finance leads, HR ops managers, and compliance officers who built reports they trust.

## Sunset timeline

Phase 1, announcement window (2026-05-20 to 2026-06-30). In-app banner, email to all tenant admins and known Legacy Reports users, migration guide published, office hours every Thursday. No functional changes.

Phase 2, active migration (2026-07-01 to 2026-08-31). Migration tool available in-app. Customer Success runs assisted-migration sessions for top 40 tenants by saved-report count. Second email reminder mid-July. New saved-report creation in Legacy Reports is disabled on 2026-08-15 (existing reports still run).

Phase 3, read-only (2026-09-01 to 2026-12-31). Legacy Reports becomes read-only. Existing reports can still be run on demand and existing scheduled exports continue to fire. No new saved reports, no edits to existing ones. Banner shifts to a countdown. Final email reminder 2026-11-01 and 2026-12-15.

Phase 4, shutdown (2027-01-15). Legacy Reports module is removed from the app. EC2 fleet is decommissioned over the following two weeks. Historical CSV exports remain in object storage per retention policy.

## What is being removed

- The Legacy Reports module UI (`/legacy-reports/*` routes in the web app).
- The Legacy Reports API surface (`api/v1/legacy-reports/*`, 23 endpoints).
- The secondary SAML login flow specific to Legacy Reports.
- The scheduled-export worker fleet for Legacy Reports (separate from the Insights export workers).
- The dedicated EC2 fleet (12 instances, two regions).
- The CSV-on-demand generator service.
- The Legacy Reports admin console used internally by Customer Success.
- The internal `legacy-reports-cli` tool used by on-call engineers.

## What is NOT being removed

This list matters. Several capabilities live near Legacy Reports and customers may assume they go away too. They do not.

- Scheduled email exports of saved reports remain available via Insights' email-export feature. The Legacy Reports email-export pipeline is being retired but the capability persists.
- CSV downloads of report results continue in Insights with broader format support (CSV, XLSX, Parquet).
- The underlying data warehouse views that Legacy Reports queried (`warehouse.hr_*` and `warehouse.payroll_*`) are not being touched. Insights queries the same views.
- Historical CSV exports already generated by Legacy Reports remain accessible via object storage for seven years from the date of generation, per the data-retention policy. A read-only download UI in Insights replaces the Legacy Reports CSV history tab.
- The Threadline public reporting API (`api/v2/reports/*`) is unaffected. It already routes through Insights.
- Customer tenant pricing is not changing as a result of this consolidation. This is a deliberate non-goal (see below).

## Migration paths

Three categories, based on internal analysis of all 4,200 saved report definitions.

Category A, direct equivalent (about 30%, around 1,260 reports). Headcount snapshots, turnover by department, time-off balances, basic payroll summaries. The migration tool converts these one-click into Insights saved reports. Scheduled exports carry over with the same cadence and recipients.

Category B, rebuild in Insights (about 55%, around 2,310 reports). Custom joins, derived columns, multi-step filters. Insights supports these capabilities but the report definition does not translate automatically. The migration tool produces a JSON export of the legacy definition and a side-by-side Insights template that the user finishes manually. Customer Success offers paired sessions.

Category C, gap (about 15%, around 630 reports). These rely on Legacy Reports features that Insights does not currently match: pivot-style cross-tab output with custom subtotals, and a specific compensation-history view that joins archived payroll runs older than five years. Plan: ship cross-tab subtotals in Insights by 2026-08-15 (committed by Insights team). For the compensation-history view, we will provide a one-time bulk JSON+CSV export per tenant on request and document the limitation.

All saved report definitions, regardless of category, are exported as JSON to each tenant's S3 bucket on 2026-12-15. Customers can re-import these into Insights or keep them as records.

## Internal cleanup

- Decommission EC2 fleet (2027-01-15 to 2027-01-31).
- Remove the secondary SAML bridge integration; revoke its credentials with the identity provider.
- Archive the `threadline-legacy-reports` source repository to read-only state with a tombstone README.
- Remove Legacy Reports references from the main app's nav config, feature-flag service, and billing line items (already $0 line items, but they need to come out).
- Update internal runbooks; retire the legacy on-call rotation.
- Internal docs: mark Legacy Reports sections in the engineering wiki as archived, link to this PRD.
- Customer-facing docs: remove Legacy Reports from the help center main nav; keep historical pages reachable via direct link for one year then redirect to Insights equivalents.

## Constraints

- No data loss. All saved report definitions exported as JSON to each tenant's S3 bucket before shutdown. All historical CSV exports remain in object storage for seven years from generation date.
- No in-flight scheduled report breaks. Scheduled exports continue running through the read-only phase. Customers who migrate their schedule to Insights have both fire (intentionally) until 2026-12-31, after which the Legacy schedule stops.
- SOX hold: any report flagged by a customer as SOX-relevant must have a certified equivalent in Insights before that tenant moves to read-only. If not, that tenant gets a per-tenant extension up to 2027-01-15 but not beyond. Compliance team owns the certification list.
- HIPAA / healthcare compliance hold: tenants in the healthcare vertical (currently 14 tenants) get a compliance review of their migrated reports by our compliance partner before read-only mode applies to them. Same hard stop at 2027-01-15.
- Auth continuity. The secondary SAML bridge stays live until 2027-01-15. No earlier removal even if internal pressure builds, because some power users depend on it daily through Phase 3.
- Pricing stability. No customer's contract pricing is adjusted as a consequence of this sunset (positive or negative) during the announcement-to-shutdown window. Renewals are handled normally on their own cadence.
- Read-only means read-only. We will not silently disable existing scheduled exports during Phase 3, even for low-usage tenants, because we want zero "I didn't notice it broke" surprises.

## Non-goals

- Not sunsetting the Insights module. Stated explicitly because internal staff have occasionally conflated "reporting consolidation" with "reporting overhaul."
- Not sunsetting the underlying warehouse views (`warehouse.hr_*`, `warehouse.payroll_*`). Insights depends on them.
- Not sunsetting the public reporting API (`api/v2/reports/*`). It routes through Insights and is unaffected.
- Not sunsetting CSV or scheduled email exports as capabilities. The Legacy pipeline goes away; Insights continues to provide both.
- Not changing tenant pricing as a result of consolidation. Deliberate, to avoid the perception of paying the same for less.
- Not migrating customers off the platform. This is a module retirement, not a product retirement.
- Not removing access to historical CSVs already generated. Seven-year retention stands.
- Not building a Legacy Reports "compatibility mode" inside Insights. We considered it. The cost outweighs the benefit given Category A coverage and the Category C plan.

## Success signals

- 100% of Category A reports migrated by 2026-08-31.
- 90% of Category B reports either migrated or explicitly abandoned by tenant by 2026-12-15.
- Zero unresolved SOX or HIPAA holds at 2027-01-15.
- Zero customer escalations citing data loss or broken scheduled reports during Phase 3.
- EC2 fleet decommissioned by 2027-01-31, removing approximately $340K of annualized cost.
- Net Promoter Score for the reporting experience flat or up at the 2027-Q1 measurement (we expect flat; up would be a pleasant surprise).
- Fewer than five tenants requesting per-tenant extensions past 2027-01-15. Anything above that suggests our migration tooling missed a category.

## Open questions and risks

- Category C compensation-history view: the one-time JSON+CSV export is acceptable for finance teams in interviews so far, but we have not validated with all 14 healthcare tenants. Risk that one needs an ongoing equivalent. Mitigation: compliance review in Phase 2.
- Cross-tab subtotals in Insights are committed for 2026-08-15. If that ships late, Category B migration in Phase 2 stalls. Mitigation: Insights team has a fallback design that ships a more limited cross-tab on time.
- The 80 monthly active users are highly engaged. Expect loud feedback even if the migration is smooth. Customer Success is staffed for it; Product Marketing has a narrative ready.
- Internal: two engineers on the Legacy Reports team are being redirected to Insights. Capacity transfer plan exists but the timing is tight against the Phase 2 cross-tab commitment.
- Object storage retention: the seven-year clock starts at CSV generation, not at sunset date. A small number of CSVs generated in 2017 and 2018 will age out during the sunset window. Communicating this proactively to affected tenants.
- We have not decided whether to keep the help-center articles for Legacy Reports indexed in search after 2027-01-15 or noindex them. Leaning noindex with redirects to Insights equivalents; final call by 2026-10-01.
